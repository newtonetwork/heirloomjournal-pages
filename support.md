# Heirloom Journal Support

Heirloom Journal is built and maintained by a small team. We read every message
and aim to respond within a few business days.

## Get help

- **Email:** [newtonetwork18@gmail.com](mailto:newtonetwork18@gmail.com)
- **Issue tracker (public):** [github.com/newtonetwork/heirloomjournal-pages/issues](https://github.com/newtonetwork/heirloomjournal-pages/issues)

When you reach out, please include:

- Device model and iOS version
- App version (Settings → About in the app)
- A short description of what you expected vs. what happened
- Steps to reproduce if you can

## Common questions

**Where is my data stored?**
Locally on your device by default. If you enable iCloud Sync, your data syncs
through your own Apple ID via iCloud / CloudKit. Heirloom Journal never
operates its own server.

**How do I export my recordings?**
Use the in-app export feature. Exports include your recordings, transcripts,
and subject metadata.

**How do I delete everything?**
Settings → Delete All Data. This is permanent and cannot be undone.
