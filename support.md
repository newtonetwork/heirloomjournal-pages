# Heirloom: Family Interviews Support

Heirloom is built and maintained by a small team. We read every message
and aim to respond within a few business days.

## Get help

- **Email:** [newtonetwork18@gmail.com](mailto:newtonetwork18@gmail.com)

When you reach out, please include:

- Device model and iOS version
- App version (Settings → About in the app)
- A short description of what you expected vs. what happened
- Steps to reproduce if you can

Please don’t send family members’ names, stories or recordings unless we ask for them to solve a problem.

## Common questions

**Where is my data stored?**
Locally on your device by default. If you turn on iCloud Sync, your notes,
transcripts, photos, interview details, and custom prompts sync through your own
private iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit). **Audio recordings never sync — they
stay on the device where they were recorded.** To keep a copy of the audio
elsewhere, use the in-app export. Heirloom never operates its own server.

**How do I export my recordings?**
Use the in-app export feature. Exports include your recordings, transcripts,
and details about each family member.

**How do I delete everything?**
Settings → Delete All Data. This is permanent and cannot be undone. It removes
everything from this device. If iCloud Sync was on, it does **not** remove the
copies in your private iCloud. To delete those too, open the iPhone Settings app →
your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage (or Manage Storage) → Heirloom,
and delete its data.
