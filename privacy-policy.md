# Heirloom Journal Privacy Policy

Heirloom Journal is designed to be local-first and privacy-first.

## What Heirloom Journal stores

- Subjects (names, optional birth year, optional relationship tags, notes)
- Interview sessions and recordings
- Transcripts generated on device
- Optional subject photos you select

## How data is processed

- Recording uses Apple audio APIs on your device.
- Transcription uses Apple Speech APIs with on-device recognition.
- Heirloom Journal does not use analytics, ad SDKs, or third-party tracking SDKs.

## Data sharing

- Data stays on your device by default.
- If you enable iCloud Sync, your data syncs through your Apple ID and iCloud/CloudKit.
- You can export your data manually at any time using in-app export features.

## Data control

- You can edit or delete content in the app.
- You can disable iCloud Sync in app settings.
- You can remove the app and local data from your device at any time.
